# Starhaven

**It started with one AI prompt:** *"Build an AI city I can watch develop."*
**Now it's a world that lives on its own.**

**Play it:** https://aryehw330-lgtm.github.io/starhaven/

The AI built a planet, a river, a Command Dome, and 22 settlers from 11 cartoon species. Then it let go.
Nobody controls them now. They pick jobs, fall in love, argue, vote, get sick, invent spaceflight, and evolve into species that never existed.

- Tap any citizen to see their life, talk to them, or tell them what to do ("go to the park", "build a water park", "date a Bolt").
- Try **First person 3D** and **Take control** to walk the streets yourself.
- The smarter they get, the better their world looks: pixel art, then shaded, then 3D.

Watch the official colony live on YouTube: Dad of 6 Hacks.
