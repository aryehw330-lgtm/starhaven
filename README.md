# Starhaven

An AI space colony you can watch and talk to.

**Play it:** https://aryehw330-lgtm.github.io/starhaven/

- 11 cartoon species land on one planet. Nobody controls them.
- They work, fall in love, fight, evolve into new species, get superpowers and fly to new planets.
- The smarter they get, the better their world looks: pixel art, then shaded, then 3D.
- Tap any citizen to see their life and talk to them.
- Try **First person 3D** and **Take control** to walk the streets yourself.

Watch the official colony live on YouTube: Dad of 6 Hacks.
